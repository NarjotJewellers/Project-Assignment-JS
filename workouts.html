Workout Plans

Full Body Workout

A full body workout hits all your major muscles in one session. Squats, push ups, and planks are a good start.

Cardio Exercise

Cardio like running, jumping jacks, or biking gets your heart rate up and burns calories.

Workout Time

Try to workout for at least 30 minutes, 3 to 4 times a week.

Benefits

Working out builds strength, helps your heart, and improves your mood.
