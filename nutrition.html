Nutrition

Healthy Food Ideas

Fill your plate with vegetables, lean protein like chicken or beans, and whole grains like rice or oats.

Healthy Snacks

Try fruit, nuts, or yogurt instead of chips or candy when you need a snack.

Drink Enough Water

Try to drink at least 8 cups of water a day to stay hydrated, more if you are working out.
